One last step: Manulife

Manulife needs you to confirm the change yourself through Singpass. It takes about two minutes. When you are done, upload a screenshot of the confirmation screen.

  1. Log in with Singpass to the Manulife customer portal at mymanulife.com.sg
  2. Go to Services on the right
  3. Select Change Representative
  4. Select I have a preferred representative
  5. Key in the code . You should see the name JEYANTHI JEGATHISAN / PIAS
  6. Tick all policies and press Next
  7. Follow the remaining authentication steps
  8. Take a screenshot of the final confirmation screen and upload it below
